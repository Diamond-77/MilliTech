Introduction to Dermatology

What is dermatology?

Dermatology is the branch of medicine that looks after the skin, hair and nails. A dermatology consultation is a chance to understand a concern properly instead of guessing.

The information on this page is general and is not a substitute for a consultation.

In general

“Many skin concerns are common. Asking early can bring peace of mind.”

Understanding Skin, Hair and Nail Health

Concerns people often ask about.

These are general examples only. They do not describe specific treatments offered at the clinic.

Skin

Rashes, itching, acne, dryness, patches or changes in skin colour.

Hair & scalp

Hair fall, thinning, dandruff or an itchy, flaky scalp.

Nails

Discoloured, brittle or painful nails, or changes in their shape.

Dermatology Consultations

What to expect.

  1. 1

    You share your concern, how long it has been there, and anything that makes it better or worse.

  2. 2

    The doctor looks at the affected area and may ask about your health, products you use and your routine.

  3. 3

    You receive an explanation and advice on the next step, which may include a test or a follow-up visit.

Role of Diagnostic Testing

Why tests matter.

In general, diagnostic tests help in three ways.

01

Confirm

Tests can help a doctor confirm what may be causing your symptoms.

02

Rule out

They can also help rule out conditions, which can bring reassurance.

03

Monitor

For ongoing conditions, regular tests help track how your health changes over time.
