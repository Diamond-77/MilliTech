FAQ

Questions before you join.

Still unsure? Call 87983 03540 or visit the gym.

01Where is RC Fitness located?

Awang Leikai, Samurou, Imphal West, Manipur 795009.

02What are the membership fees?

Monthly ₹1,500. Three months ₹2,250, six months ₹4,500 and one year ₹9,000 at the listed 50% discount. Admission is ₹1,000.

03Does RC Fitness offer student membership?

Yes, for students up to Class XII: ₹1,500 for three months, ₹3,000 for six months and ₹6,000 for a year.

04Is a student ID card compulsory?

Yes. A student ID card is required for student membership.

05What fitness programmes are available?

Bodybuilding, weight training, cardio, a fat loss programme, aerobic, Zumba, plyometric training, battling ropes, personal training, and diet and nutrition guidance.

06Does the gym offer personal training?

Yes, as separately priced packages of one, three, six or twelve months.

07What are the personal trainer fees?

₹3,000 for one month, ₹8,100 for three months (10% off), ₹15,300 for six months (15% off) and ₹28,800 for a year (20% off).

08Are aerobic and Zumba programmes available?

Yes. Aerobic / Zumba only is ₹600, Aerobic + Zumba + Gym is ₹800, and plyometric exercise with music is ₹600.

09What are the gym's opening hours?

Summer: 5:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Winter: 6:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Call to check before you visit.

10Is the gym open on Sundays?

No, the gym is closed on Sunday.

11How can I enquire about joining?

Call 87983 03540, message @rc_fitnessamurou on Instagram, or visit the gym in Awang Leikai, Samurou.

12Are there different membership durations?

Yes: monthly, three months, six months and one year. Students can choose three months, six months or one year.
