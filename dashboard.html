Follow up now

Before you dial
  1. Breathe, go slow. Slow sounds confident. Stuttering is fine, rushing is not.
  2. Open with a question about them. How do most new customers find you right now?
  3. Then shut up and listen. Ask one more: What happens when someone searches your name on Google?
  4. Their problem, not your price. Missed calls, orders lost in WhatsApp chats, no page to show trust. Price only when they ask.
  5. Ask for a tiny next step. Can I send you a 1-minute demo on WhatsApp?
