Who we are

This website is run by MilliTech, a web service agency based in Imphal West, Manipur, India. Contact: MilliTech, Imphal West, Manipur, India. WhatsApp +91 60092 71012, email millitech.official@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Claim form. Your name, business, WhatsApp number and what you need. The form does not send these to a server. It opens WhatsApp with a message containing them. We only receive them if you press send.

How we use it

To reply to you, call you back, prepare a quote, deliver and support your project, and understand how people use this website so we can improve it. We do not sell your data or use it for third-party advertising.

How long we keep it

Project conversations are kept while we work together and for up to 2 years after, so we can support your site. Ask us any time and we will delete what we hold, unless we must keep it for tax or legal reasons.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, and withdraw consent for analytics at any time, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Message us on WhatsApp or email us.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
